Waterfront Construction in Grosse Pointe Woods

Grosse Pointe Woods is the only one of the five Grosse Pointe cities without Lake St. Clair shoreline of its own, though the city owns Lake Front Park in nearby St. Clair Shores, a residents-only park with a boardwalk and dockage. That means seawall and dock work is unusual inside the city itself.

What Woods homeowners do need is the work that surrounds a home: new patios and driveways, decks, drainage and grading, demolition of old garages or slabs, and custom steel railings and gates. We also help Woods residents who own or manage waterfront property elsewhere on Lake St. Clair, from a lake home to a boat well.

Grosse Pointe Woods at a Glance

  • Grosse Pointe Woods is in Wayne County and has no direct Lake St. Clair shoreline.
  • The city owns Lake Front Park in St. Clair Shores.

Permits and Local Rules in Grosse Pointe Woods

Because Grosse Pointe Woods has no Lake St. Clair shoreline of its own, most projects here are governed by the city building department: permits and inspections for decks, driveways, patios, demolition and grading. If you also own waterfront property elsewhere, state and federal permits may apply to that work. See our Michigan permit guide.
