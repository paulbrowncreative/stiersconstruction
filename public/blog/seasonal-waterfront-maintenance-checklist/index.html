Spring and Fall Waterfront Maintenance Checklist for Michigan Homeowners

A seasonal checklist for seawalls, docks, hoists, pilings, decks and concrete on Michigan waterfront properties, for spring and fall.

Waterfront property rewards regular attention. A twice-a-year walkthrough catches small issues before they become expensive. Use this checklist each spring and fall, and keep your photos in a folder.

Key Takeaways

  • Walk your waterfront twice a year: after the thaw and before freeze-up.
  • Take dated photos so you can compare year to year.
  • Small repairs before winter prevent big ones in spring.
  • Book contractors early. Spring is busy.

Spring Checklist (After the Thaw)

Seawall

  • Sight along the top for leaning or bowing.
  • Look for new sinkholes, voids or settling behind the wall.
  • Check caps and seams for cracks and openings.
  • Check the wall ends for erosion.
  • Clear and check drainage.

Dock and Pilings

  • Check decking for damage and loose boards.
  • Look for pilings that have shifted, tilted or lifted over winter. See winter damage.
  • Check hardware, gangways and connections.

Boat Hoist

  • Inspect cables, motor, welds and frame before loading a boat. See the hoist maintenance checklist.

Deck and Concrete

  • Look for cracks, heaving, loose boards and fasteners.
  • Reseal or restain wood as needed.

Depth

  • Check that your slip or boat well still has enough depth. See dredging 101.

Fall Checklist (Before Freeze-up)

  • Repeat the seawall check and repair cracks and openings you can before winter.
  • Clear leaves and debris from drains and drainage paths.
  • Follow manufacturer guidance for hoist and dock winter preparation.
  • Check piling covers.
  • Take photos of everything for spring comparison.
  • Book off-season repairs while contractors have more room in their schedules.

After Storms and High-Water Events

Walk the shoreline after major storms and high-water periods. See Great Lakes water levels for why.

Keep a Simple Log

Note the date, what you saw and photos. If something changes, you will have a record. If you sell, it helps buyers and inspectors too.

Mid-Season Quick Checks

  • Walk the wall after heavy rain and note where water goes.
  • Check dock boards and hardware for anything loose.
  • Run the hoist through its travel.
  • Clear debris from drains and boat wells.

After a Big Storm

  1. Stay off unstable ground and away from the water’s edge while conditions are rough.
  2. Once it is safe, walk the shoreline and photograph any damage.
  3. Check for new erosion, displaced docks and debris.
  4. Contact your insurance provider if there is damage that may be covered.
  5. Call a contractor for structural issues.

Build a Contractor List

Have contacts ready for a marine contractor, an electrician for hoist work, and your local building department. When something happens, you will know who to call. Save the number for Stier’s Construction: 586-703-7214.

Budget for Maintenance

A little money each year on cap sealing, drainage, cables and coatings can prevent large repairs. Keep records of what you spend. Read how to pay for a waterfront project if you are planning a larger job.

When to Call a Professional

Call sooner if the wall leans, soil sinks, the dock moves, cables fray or you see cracks in welds. Stier’s Construction covers seawalls, docks, hoists, pilings, concrete and decks. Request a free quote.
