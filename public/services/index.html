Not Sure Where to Start?

Most waterfront jobs involve more than one service. A failing seawall may also mean redoing the patio behind it and hauling away old concrete, and a new dock may call for pilings, a hoist and a permit. Start with what you can see:

  • The Wall Leans or Soil Is SinkingSeawall repair or replacement, including caps, seams and drainage.
  • Your Boat Touches BottomBoat well, slip and canal dredging to restore depth.
  • A Dock or Lift Shows WearDock repair and re-decking, plus pilings and boat hoist service.
  • Old Slabs, Garages or ShedsConcrete removal, demolition and new flatwork.
  • Railings, Gates or Custom MetalShop and mobile welding and fabrication.
