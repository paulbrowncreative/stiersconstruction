Four Finishes

Brushed

A textured, non-slip surface that enhances safety and gives a clean, natural look. Well suited to driveways, walkways and patios.

Exposed Aggregate

Decorative stones or pebbles show on the surface for a striking, durable finish. Good for patios, driveways and walkways.

Smooth Trowel

A sleek, polished surface for floors, slabs, and indoor or outdoor projects. A classic finish that is easy to maintain and gives a refined, professional look.

Stamped

Mimics stone, brick or tile with the strength of concrete. Endless design possibilities for patios, driveways and walkways.

Concrete: Common Questions

Which Finish Is Best for Slip Resistance?

A brushed finish gives a textured, non-slip surface, which is why it is popular for driveways, walkways and patios.

What Is Exposed Aggregate Concrete?

Concrete with the top layer removed so decorative stones or pebbles show. It is durable and looks distinctive on patios, driveways and walkways.

Can Concrete Look Like Stone or Brick?

Yes. Stamped concrete mimics the look of stone, brick or tile while keeping the durability of concrete.

Do You Remove Old Concrete?

Yes. We break it apart, handle the pieces safely, haul it away and leave the site ready for new construction or landscaping.
